# Social and Email Infrasystem Architecture (v1.0)
**DevCenterPoint**

---

## 1. Social Presence Priorities

| Tier | Platforms | Target Handle | Focus / Purpose |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 1 (Core)** | **LinkedIn**<br>**GitHub**<br>**Facebook**<br>**YouTube** | `@DevCenterPoint` | B2B lead generation, engineering credibility, technical video walkthroughs, company status. |
| **Tier 2 (Secondary)** | **X (Twitter)**<br>**Instagram** | `@DevCenterPoint` | Developer updates, behind-the-scenes engineering design, brand announcements. |
| **Tier 3 (Reserved)** | **TikTok / Others** | `@DevCenterPoint` | Register defensively to safeguard namespace; activate only when campaign-relevant. |

---

## 2. Inbound Email Routing (Shared Aliases)

Instead of paying for individual mailboxes for every function, operate unified aliases routed to centralized founder/team inboxes:

* `hello@devcenterpoint.com` — General inquiries, public correspondence.
* `sales@devcenterpoint.com` — Inbound prospective client leads and RFP submissions.
* `support@devcenterpoint.com` — Active client ticketing and maintenance requests.
* `billing@devcenterpoint.com` — Invoicing, vendor payments, and accounting.
* `security@devcenterpoint.com` — Vulnerability reporting and compliance verification.
* `careers@devcenterpoint.com` — Talent acquisition and engineering hiring.
