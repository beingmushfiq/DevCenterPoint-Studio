# What DevCenterPoint Is NOT (v1.0)
**DevCenterPoint Boundary & Qualification Doctrine**

To preserve enterprise valuation, engineering quality, and market positioning, DevCenterPoint explicitly rejects the following models:

---

## 1. Not a Generic Freelance Agency
We do not bid on gig platforms or sell low-margin commodity labor. We engage as engineering partners delivering system-level outcomes.

## 2. Not a Cheap Web-Development Shop
We do not build throwaway brochure sites on bloated visual page builders. We engineer reliable web, cloud, and mobile architectures.

## 3. Not an AI Wrapper Company
We do not re-package thin API endpoints and market them as "magic AI revolutions." We integrate machine intelligence into verifiable operational workflows.

## 4. Not a Template Reseller
We do not re-skin purchased third-party themes and pass them off as proprietary systems.

## 5. Not a Digital Marketing-Only Agency
We are software engineers, systems architects, and product builders. We do not offer ad management, SEO spamming, or social media handling disconnected from software systems.

## 6. Not a Hardware Reseller
We focus on software, systems integration, and cloud architecture.

## 7. Not a Shop That Accepts Every Project
We vet client viability, data security requirements, operational readiness, and scope sanity before signing contracts. Saying no to bad fits preserves our team bandwidth for serious software engineering.
