# Portfolio Categorization Strategy (v1.0)
**DevCenterPoint**

To present high institutional credibility without misrepresenting historical facts, all work is divided into four strictly defined tiers:

---

## Tier A — Company Case Studies
* **Definition:** Projects contracted, architected, and delivered directly under DevCenterPoint.
* **Placement:** Primary `Work` section on `devcenterpoint.com`.
* **Standard:** Thorough architectural breakdown, problem statement, solution stack, metrics, and technical diagrams.

## Tier B — Founder Portfolio
* **Definition:** Production-grade projects completed personally by Aayan prior to the formal establishment of DevCenterPoint.
* **Placement:** Attributed as *Prior Selected Work by Founding Team* or hosted on personal profile.
* **Standard:** Transparent attribution to establish technical competency without claiming DevCenterPoint corporate history.

## Tier C — Commercial Products
* **Definition:** Internal IP, developer tooling, or SaaS platforms built and owned by DevCenterPoint.
* **Placement:** `Products` section on `devcenterpoint.com`.

## Tier D — Technical Experiments
* **Definition:** Prototypes, hackathon proofs-of-concept, and research tests.
* **Placement:** Personal GitHub (`beingmushfiq`) only. Never displayed on the primary commercial website.
